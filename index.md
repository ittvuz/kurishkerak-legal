# kurishkerak-shorts

**kurishkerak-shorts** is a small private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel [Ko'rish kerak](https://www.youtube.com/@korishkerak). Its only purpose is to automatically upload the channel owner's own videos (YouTube Shorts) to that same channel through the YouTube Data API.

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other users.
